# 📖 Steve's Journal

> *The collected writings, discoveries, and stories of Steve.*

---

## ✦ About This Journal

Before there were machines, plugins, and systems built to make life easier, there was Steve. Through years of exploration, experimentation, failure, and discovery, Steve uncovered ways to solve problems that had plagued the world for generations. These are his stories.

Each entry tells the story behind one of the discoveries that would eventually become part of a larger collection of tools used throughout the Minecraft world. Some discoveries were born from necessity. Some were born from curiosity.

And some... were simply accidents.

---

## 📚 Journal Entries

### ✦ Entry 1 — RS-MobStand

Steve spent years butchering animals by day and fighting monsters by night. His journal entry reveals how he discovered a way to give an armor stand a purpose — allowing it to take on the work he had grown tired of doing himself.

### ✦ Entry 2 — RS-Disenchanter

Steve couldn't accept that the magic within an enchanted item should simply disappear when the item was destroyed. Years of research and experimentation eventually led him to discover a way to separate enchantments from their vessels and preserve them within books.

But not all magic was willing to leave quietly.

[📖 Read the Journal Entry](stories/02%29%20DisEnchanter.md)

---

### ✦ Entry 3 — RS-MobCages

After discovering how to make an armor stand carry out his work, Steve soon encountered another problem — moving the creatures he had collected.

Ordinary pens and fences could keep creatures contained, but they did little to help when Steve needed to move them. His experiments eventually led to a strange discovery: a cage that could hold a creature within its magic and release it whenever it was needed.

But Steve soon discovered that capturing a creature was only the beginning.
